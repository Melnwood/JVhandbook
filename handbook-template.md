# JV Country Handbook — Template

*A working template for what a finished national handbook contains, built by reading real ones.*
*Sources so far: 🇺🇦 Ukraine (Epokha, 2017) · 🇸🇰 Slovakia (TCKompas). Grows as more come in.*

---

## The one organizing idea

Every national handbook is built around **two identities** that fork almost every policy:

- **First-culture** (the national — Ukrainian, Slovak…) **vs. Second-culture** (foreign missionary — US, Czech…)
- **Employee AND missionary** ("we answer to two authorities — the Lord and our earthly leader")

JV policy governs second-culture staff; the national handbook exists mainly to make things clear for **first-culture staff and the local legal context**. When reading any handbook, keep asking: *does this differ by culture, and who is this rule really for?*

---

## The order is different from the NFP handbook

A national handbook leads with **who they are**, then explains **who they partner with**:

> 1.1 Mission / Vision / Values → **1.2 National organization — name & history** → **1.3 Josiah Venture — name & history** → 1.4 Style of Ministry → …

Their own organization comes **first (1.2)**; Josiah Venture comes **after (1.3)**. The US/NFP handbook is JV-first; the country handbook is **us-first, then JV**. Your content sheet already encodes exactly this order.

## What the content sheet tells us

`JV Handbook - content.xlsx (Overall)` is a section-by-section **matrix** of the master **Country Handbook** table of contents against what each country actually kept — columns **Slovakia (Kompas) · Ukraine · Czech (Kam)**, an **X** where that country included the section. It's the empirical backbone for this template: for every section it shows whether it's used by **all three** (→ core / Universal), by **some**, or by **one** (→ likely local or optional). A few reads:

- **Shared by all three:** 1.1 Mission (+ Vision/Mission/Values), 3.3 Vacations, plus the JV-identity and emergency sections.
- **One country only:** Spiritual Gifts (Ukraine), Career Program (Czech) — candidates for *optional*, not *core*.
- **Marked optional in the sheet itself:** "Chapter 5 — Expenses/Reimbursements *doesn't have to be in there*."
- Chapter renumbering vs. the NFP master (e.g. Ch. 6 becomes **Ministry Team Development**) — the country TOC is its own spine, not a copy of the NFP one.

## The four buckets

### 1 · Core things every handbook talks about  *(the shared spine of topics)*
Both Ukraine and Slovakia cover these, whoever writes them:

| Topic area | UA | SK |
|---|---|---|
| Identity — vision, mission, values; who JV is + who the national org is | ✓ | ✓ |
| The dual identity — employee/missionary, first/second-culture | ✓ | ✓ |
| Governance & structure — legal bodies, board, leadership, Uplink | ✓ | ✓ |
| Communications & online — websites, email, calendars, drive, BIO pages, branding | ✓ | ✓ |
| Work & rest — schedules, holidays, vacation, comp days, leaves, home assignment, development, retreats | ✓ | ✓ |
| Conduct & standards — faith statement, LGBT position, harassment, conciliation | ✓ | ✓ |
| Finances — support raising, salary, expenses/reimbursement, reporting | ✓ | ✓ |
| Emergencies / crisis | full section | light |

### 2 · Taken from the US/JV handbook to talk about Josiah Venture  *(inherited → "Universal")*
Comes down from JV, meant to read the same in every country:

- JV **vision** — "a movement of God among the youth… that finds its home in the local church and transforms society" (both echo it)
- JV **mission** — "equip young leaders to fulfill Christ's commission through the local church"
- JV **core values / key convictions** (UA copies verbatim; **SK rewrote its own** — see divergence note)
- **Josiah Venture's name** (King Josiah), **JV history**, the **JV Covenant**
- **Style of Ministry** (incarnational + multiplying; the 5 challenges) and **Strategy** (5 phases)
- **Statement of Faith**; **Spiritual Gifts** paper; **LGBT position** paper; **Ministry Lanes**
- **JV NFP Board**, **European JV Council**
- Standards of conduct, harassment/discrimination, sexual conduct, Christian conciliation
- **Emergency / evacuation / hostage** framework
- JV-wide structures & benefits: **Uplink**, **Selah** (JV rest lodge), **JV Fall conference**, **professional & executive development** tiers, home-assignment concept, Czech training center
- JV systems: **JVHub**, **JV Design Center**, JV financial-report videos, **JV BIO pages**, josiahventure.com

### 3 · Key pieces First-Culture staff must know about engaging with JVNFP  *(the interface)*
The rules of the relationship between a national staff member and **Josiah Venture NFP** (the US entity):

- **Two authorities** — the Lord + your earthly leader; if the national org ever leaves the JV partnership, the handbook is void (UA)
- **Raise personal support through JV**, paid as JV **contract employees / independent consultants** (UA: Independent Consulting Service Agreement)
- **US-law money rules** — JV funds only for charitable/religious/educational use; no budget variance without JV's written approval (UA)
- **Support "track record"** → salary is released on a scale as monthly support proves in; salary vs. ministry split (UA)
- **Cedarstone** back-office; **wire transfers** every 2 months; US/Canada donor processing (UA)
- **US-IRS-style reimbursement** — receipts, itemization, mileage at/below US IRS rates (UA)
- **Brand & representation control** — may only use **JV-designed materials** for US communication; **may not create own materials without JV approval** (SK, strongly)
- **JV BIO pages** staff must keep updated (SK: contact Kim Shepperson)
- **JV financial reporting** via JV instructional videos (SK)
- **JV Fall conference obligatory** for JV missionaries; **Selah** access; **executive development** eligibility measured "in JV ministry" (SK)
- **Home assignment** = engagement trip to the home country, cleared with the leader (both)
- HR, transfers, and exit run through the **JV HR Department** (UA)

### 4 · What they write themselves  *(local → "All National")*
Country-authored, unique to them:

- Their **own vision/mission/values wording** — **SK wrote distinct values** (Dependence on God · Love through grace & truth · Excellence · Personal Growth · Faithfulness/Honesty), *different from JV's five*
- **National org name meaning & legal name** (UA: Епоха; "Epokha 2.2" = 2 Timothy 2:2)
- **National org history & founding**
- **Local legal governance** & named bodies (UA: General Gathering / Правління / President / Наглядова рада; SK: General Assembly / Board / L-team / X-team / Uplink)
- **Named leaders, offices, addresses, contacts** (SK: 4 offices + office leaders; UA: Executive Board members)
- **Local systems & tools** (SK: TCK "orange system" for vacation, TCK server, Google drive/calendars, project names — KPM, SPM, KECY, FUSION, EXIT, EDGE, ADVANCE — email domains, software admin)
- **Local holidays & vacation law/amounts** (UA: 24 days by law, Ukrainian holidays; SK: 15 days +5 after 10 yrs, ~11 holidays, medical 12 days, bereavement 5 days)
- **Local leave specifics** — comp days, **days of solitude** (SK, quarterly), **Israel trips** (SK)
- **Local taxes / pension / labor book** (UA: трудова книга, Ukrainian pension fund)
- **Local salary levels & amounts** (UA: intern $100/mo, ministry target $800, the support-release scale)
- **Local emergency context** (UA: war, political upheaval, avoid partisan social media)
- **Local operational protocols** (SK: 48-hour email rule, phone etiquette, meeting cadence, work attendance, the FAQ)

## What the two handbooks reveal (the divergence)

- **Ukraine hugs the master.** Epokha inherits JV content nearly verbatim, even writing "See 1.1.b" — it reads like the JV master with Ukrainian specifics dropped in. Heavy on the **JVNFP financial machinery** (Cedarstone, wire transfers, US IRS rules, hostage policy).
- **Slovakia localizes hard.** TCKompas reorganizes the whole handbook around its own operational reality — **Uplink**, four offices, online systems, days of solitude, an FAQ — and even **rewrote the core values**. Lighter on JV boilerplate, heavier on "how we actually run day to day."

**Implication for the master:** the system needs to mark clearly which sections are **Universal** (must read the same everywhere — e.g. Statement of Faith, LGBT position) vs. where a country may legitimately diverge. SK's rewritten values would show up as a **"departure"** today — exactly the kind of thing leadership should see and decide on.

## Checklist for the next handbook (repeat per country)

For each finished handbook, capture:
- [ ] Which JV content they kept **verbatim** vs. **adapted** vs. **dropped** (bucket 2)
- [ ] Any place they **diverged** from a Universal section (flag for leadership)
- [ ] Their **JVNFP interface** specifics — support, money, brand, reporting (bucket 3)
- [ ] Everything **locally authored** — governance, people, holidays, amounts, systems (bucket 4)
- [ ] How they handle **first- vs second-culture** differences
- [ ] Local **structure** (some reorganize the chapters entirely, like SK)
